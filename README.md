# interpretable-dss-cancer

Code accompanying the doctoral thesis **"Interpretable Decision Support Systems for Early
Cancer Diagnosis and Personalized Treatment"** (Johan S. Peña-Campos, Universitat
Politècnica de Catalunya / Pontificia Universidad Javeriana).

The thesis spans two stages of the cancer care pathway, and this repository is organized
the same way. The two stages are complementary but not chained: they concern different
diseases and different data regimes, and the output of one is not an input to the other.

| Directory | Thesis | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`nobsp/`](nobsp/) | Chapter 3 — Nonlinear Oblique Subspace Projections | **available** |
| [`gastric-risk/`](gastric-risk/) | Chapter 4 — population-scale gastric cancer risk prediction | **available** |
| [`adaptive-therapy/`](adaptive-therapy/) | Chapters 5 and 6 — MPC for adaptive therapy in mCRPC | **available** |

---

## Reproducibility, honestly stated

**`adaptive-therapy` is reproducible in full.** It depends on no restricted data. Every
parameter value and every scenario configuration is in `adaptive-therapy/params/`.

**`gastric-risk` will publish code but not data.** The clinical records it was trained on
comprise longitudinal diagnosis and procedure sequences for 4.9 million patients of the
Colombian health system, together with municipality of residence. Removing direct
identifiers does not make such records anonymous, and they are not the authors' data to
distribute: they were accessed under ethics approval FM-CIE-030920 and remain under the
custody of the issuing institutions. Requests for access should be directed to that
custodian.

To keep the pipeline runnable, a generator emits a **synthetic cohort** in the same
schema, with the same array shapes and class ratio, drawing codes from the public ICD-10
and CUPS catalogues. It reproduces the *mechanics* of the pipeline, not the statistics of
the real cohort. Results obtained on synthetic data are not comparable with those reported
in the thesis.

**Trained weights are not distributed.** A model fitted to patient records can in
principle disclose information about them. They are available on reasonable request,
subject to the terms of the ethics approval.

**This code is not byte-for-byte what produced the thesis results.** The differences are
presentational, selective, and one verified-inert correction. They are listed in
[`CHANGES-FROM-THESIS.md`](CHANGES-FROM-THESIS.md).

## A caveat on the treatment results

The simulation studies of Chapters 5 and 6 are a **proof of concept**. The reported
extensions of time to progression, the reductions in cumulative drug exposure and the long
control horizons are hypotheses generated by the adopted models, not dosing
recommendations. They rest on assumptions of full state observability, noise-free
measurement, fixed phenotypes and patient-independent parameters, none of which a clinical
deployment would satisfy.
